The Journey

From idea to Imperion

The milestones that shaped our product and our team.

January 2024

The Spark

Shandhip Prince identified a gap in accessible AI-powered image editing. The first lines of code were written in a dorm room with a vision to make professional editing available to everyone.

March 2024

First Prototype

The team expanded to include AI researchers and UX designers. The first working prototype with background removal launched to a small beta group of 100 users.

June 2024

Public Beta

Imperion opened to the public with 10 AI-powered tools. The response was overwhelming — 5,000 users signed up within the first week.

October 2024

Imperion Studio Launch

The full-featured editor launched with real-time collaboration, style transfer, and 4K export. Featured on Product Hunt as #1 Product of the Day.

January 2025

Community & Growth

Launched the community gallery, reached 15K active creators, and introduced AI image generation. The team grew to include dedicated community managers.

May 2025

Today & Beyond

Imperion continues to evolve with cutting-edge AI features, a thriving community, and a commitment to making creativity accessible to all.

What We Stand For

Our values

The principles that guide every decision we make.

🎨

Creativity First

We believe everyone is creative. Our tools exist to remove barriers, not create them. Every feature is designed to amplify human imagination.

🤖

AI as a Partner

AI should assist, not replace. We build intelligent tools that enhance human creativity while keeping the artist in control.

🌍

Accessibility

Professional-grade editing should be available to everyone, regardless of skill level, device, or budget. No gatekeeping.

🔒

Privacy by Design

Your images are yours. We process everything locally when possible and never use your data for training without explicit consent.

💬

Community Driven

We build with our community, not for them. Feedback shapes our roadmap and our gallery celebrates every creator's work.

⚡

Relentless Innovation

We never stop pushing boundaries. Our team is constantly researching, experimenting, and shipping new AI capabilities.
